file 01 / how it plays

Your sticks are your hands.

  • Moves change with you. Square, circle and triangle do something different for every tie, hip height and hand direction. The bar under the mat shows what they do right now. With coach on, the filled ones would land.
  • The arch. Get a body lock and your back arches. Pick a slam before the lock slips. Caught in one? Mash and drop your hips.
  • Gas. Everything costs conditioning and the ceiling sinks as the match goes. Stand easy with no ties to get it back.
  • Ways to lose. Step past the white line, stay on your back for two seconds, fall 15 points behind, or be behind when the clock dies.
